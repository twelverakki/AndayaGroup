SUBMENU BREAKDOWN FOR SUPERADMIN & OWNER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Menu Induk: Organisasi & Pengguna (organization)

1. Submenu 1: Pengguna & Staff (/organization/staff)
   - Target DB: users, outlet_staff, business_owners, manager_pins
   - Fitur:
     * Master tabel pengguna: Nama, Email Gmail (@gmail.com), Role, Unit Bisnis, Outlet, Status.
     * Floating capsule header & tombol pagination bulat.
     * Filter: Berdasarkan Unit Bisnis (Holding filter) & Role (Owner, Manager, Staff, Admin Gudang).
     * Modal Tambah Pengguna Baru (Password mode & PIN Kasir mode).
     * Modal Fast Reset PIN Kasir 6-digit via <InputOTP>.
     * Quick Action: Ubah status (Active / Inactive / Suspended).

2. Submenu 2: Unit Bisnis & Capability (/organization/businesses)
   - Target DB: businesses
   - Fitur:
     * Master kartu/tabel 4 unit bisnis (JnA Mart, Bakso Kang Gemoy, Yasaka, Gorengan).
     * Modal Tambah / Edit Unit Bisnis.
     * Toggle Capability Flags (The Lean Odoo Way):
       - has_pos (Aktivasi POS Barcode / Fast Grid)
       - has_manufacturing (Aktivasi BOM Resep & Batch Dapur)
       - has_logistics_hub (Aktivasi Pengiriman Antar-Gudang)
       - has_eod_usage (Aktivasi Lembar EOD Kasir)

3. Submenu 3: Cabang & Outlet (/organization/outlets)
   - Target DB: outlets, businesses
   - Fitur:
     * Master daftar cabang/outlet fisik seluruh unit bisnis holding.
     * Informasi: Nama Outlet, Unit Bisnis Induk, Alamat, Jumlah Staff Terdaftar.
     * Modal Tambah Outlet Baru & Edit Outlet.
     * Penugasan Penanggung Jawab Outlet (Manager Assignment).

4. Submenu 4: Jejak Audit & Otorisasi (/organization/audit-logs)
   - Target DB: override_logs, audit_logs
   - Fitur:
     * Riwayat otorisasi Manager PIN Override (Void transaksi kasir, approval opname/wastage).
     * Filter rentang tanggal, cabang outlet, dan jenis otorisasi.
     * Informasi audit trail lengkap untuk investigasi anti-fraud.
